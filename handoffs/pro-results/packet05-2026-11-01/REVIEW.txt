WHAT THE REPORT PROVES
For uniformly positive, piecewise constant scalar permittivity separated by
fixed C3 smooth interfaces, on patches without edges, corners or junctions,
local L2 electric fields satisfying curl curl E=F in L2 and div(epsilon E)=0
gain piecewise H2 regularity on smaller patches, with quantitative local
bounds. The argument first obtains H(curl) by a mollified Caccioppoli estimate,
then H1 control of curl E, and decomposes E into a gradient plus an H2 field.
A flattened scalar transmission problem supplies the remaining H3 potential.
Tangential difference quotients preserve the two half-spaces; the equation
recovers normal derivatives in the correct order. Its weak formulation already
encodes the transmission condition; classical traces are not assumed at the
start. Piecewise H2 extension and Sobolev embedding give continuous one-sided
representatives and uniform bounds. The magnetic argument is consistent with
the stated Maxwell sign convention.

For frequencies k_j -> k_* != 0 and electric L2 convergence, the difference
equation contains the additional forcing
  (k_j^2-k_*^2) epsilon E_*.
It tends to zero in L2 on each fixed patch. The same local estimate therefore
gives uniform convergence on each material side of fixed collars, provided
the material agrees on an OPEN neighborhood of those collars and a finite
fixed chart cover is available. Estimates near changing rounded tips need
not be uniform. No regularity estimate is claimed across the sharp vertices.

The pointwise convention is the maximum of the magnitudes of all incident
continuous material-side representatives. For finitely many closed material
branches it is upper semicontinuous; it need not be continuous. On the compact
observation ball, each relevant branch is restricted to K intersect closure
of its phase. This gives an attained maximum. Positive-volume high-value sets
survive deletion of representative/interface null sets. L2 convergence to an
essentially unbounded sharp field gives eventual high values by Chebyshev's
inequality. Finite intersection of eventual tails gives ONE rounding index
working at every tip, with strict collar/peak separation. The connected-
component localization proof uses the continuous radius and upper
semicontinuity and does not assume the superlevel set is open.
