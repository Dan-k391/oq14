   4/R and step bound |h|<R/8. All required translated segments lie where the
   outer cutoff equals one. The translated equations hold on the common open
   domain where both original and shifted fields are defined.
3. Compact H(curl) testing follows by zero extension of supported fields and
   mollification. The proof does not presuppose the H1 regularity it derives.

   inequality yields A <= 2 max(Re(k^2),0) Q + 4D. The signs and coercivity
   constants are correct, including the complex-frequency case.
5. The compactly supported div-curl identity first bounds the H1 norm of a
   cutoff H. This controls the lower-order tangential quotient independently
   of the differentiated curl being estimated. Applying the energy identity
   to that quotient then closes the highest-order estimate without circularity.
6. With M=1+d, L=4/R and B=sqrt(2 max(Re(k^2),0)+64/R^2), the mixed bound is
   sqrt(M) B (|k| M ||E|| + L ||H||), and the H-only bound is
   (M B^2 + sqrt(M) B L) ||H||. The norms use physical vector L2 sums.

   contrast. It still requires a bound on the fields themselves. The report
   treats k=0 separately and does not infer div H=0 from a zero frequency.
   It does not claim curved-interface regularity or attainment of maxima.
