# Counted subspaces of the actual device: what the Lean statement asserts (author scope note, October 24)

Author scope note by the channel proof agent for the final counted-subspace theorem `OQ14.RestrictedTEActualSquare.actual_counted_subspaces_square` (lean/RestrictedTEActualSquare.lean, reviews/restricted_te_actual_square.md) and the modules below it: `RestrictedTEActualWindow` (window and geometry, `actual_counted_subspaces_all` for the unbounded and disk-bounded symmetric sections), `RestrictedTEActualCounting` (diagonal transfer, `IsCounted`), `RestrictedTEL1Assembly` (reduction of `L1Interface`, parity tail), `RestrictedTEActualFields` (composition with abstract linear plane fields), `RestrictedTEActualCore` (the inside-core actual fields, their convergence, the twisted counted-subspace theorem), `ChannelScatteringMap` and `ChannelMaterialThroughput` (unit throughput of the actual material device), `ChannelUnitThroughput`, `ChannelFarFluxExpansion`, `ChannelScaledFields`, `ChannelCesaroThroughput` (the energy identity), `RestrictedTEPartnerSmallness`, `RestrictedTEPartnerTransfer`, `WeightedRemainderSchur` (the effective unit-ness of the limiting block). Nothing here upgrades a manuscript PROVED label.

## 1. The manuscript statement being approached

Theorem `uc:counterexample` (PROVED in the manuscript): fix `k0 > 0`, `eps_+ > 1`, `L > 0`, `0 < tau < 1`, `0 < eta < delta`; for every positive integer `N` there is a finite piecewise smooth real scalar dielectric with `1 <= eps_r <= eps_+`, vacuum exterior, whose physical incoming space contains an `N`-dimensional counted subspace through the same square `D`; the selected outgoing map is the complete scattering map, every vector has unit power throughput, passage at least `eta` times the power, absolute bypass at most `delta` times the power, all plane fluxes integrable. Definition `pr:count` of a counted subspace: transmission `||Ta||^2 >= tau ||a||^2`, passage `P_D(a) >= eta ||Ta||^2`, absolute bypass `int_{z=0 \ D} |S_z(a)| <= delta ||a||^2`.

## 2. What the Lean statement asserts for the actual dielectric

Under the field hypotheses of section 3 (`actual_counted_subspaces_of_fields`; the bare form with `HomogeneousFlux` and `L1Interface` is `actual_counted_subspaces_square`), for every `eps_+ > 1`, every half-side `L > 0` of the fixed square `Dsq L = {|x| < L, |y| < L}` (the same square for every `N`), every `N`, every `0 < eta < delta` and every `tau <= 1` (wave number normalized to `k = 1`), there are
- a shell ratio `q` with `1 < q^2 <= eps_+` and a sphere contrast `chi > 0` with `1 + chi <= eps_+` (explicitly `q = sqrt((1 + eps_+)/2)`, `chi = min(1, eps_+ - 1)`);
- an explicit doubled device: `N` small spheres of radius `rho a_n` at height `h a_n`, `a_n = (q^n)^-1`, above the points `(-L, y_l)` of the left edge of the square, `y_l = (l+1)/(N+1) L` on a fixed segment inside the edge (separation `L/(N+1)`, a per-`N` positive constant; no feature size is bounded uniformly), their half-turn images above the right edge; the local normal `(1, 0)` at the original points points into the square and the normal `(-1, 0)` at the images points into the square as well (the section is symmetric under `x -> -x`); the device is embedded in the actual paired-shell stack `shell q hq n r` with shell index `r`;
- an orthonormal family `A : Fin N -> Index ->0 C` of `N` finite restricted TE inputs (the interpolation family) spanning an `N`-dimensional subspace,
such that:
- Permittivity (proved, no hypothesis): for every gain index `n` and all large shell indices `r`, the dielectric function of the actual device (`1 +` the susceptibility of the shell plus that of the spheres, disjoint supports) takes values in `[1, eps_+]` at every point (`device_permittivity_bounds`).
- Transmission (proved, no hypothesis on `Fact`; the effective block is a unit for large `n` by the partner-device smallness): for all large `n` and then all large `r`, the scattering map `actualScatter` (the amplitude of the complete outgoing field in `L2(sphere, C^3)`, normalized like the input) satisfies `||actualScatter x||^2 = physicalPower x = ||input x||^2` for every finite input `x` (exact unit throughput, from the weak Maxwell system of the actual material solution, the sphere-flux constancy of the lossless field, the far expansion with the oscillating cross terms and the Cesaro lemma). So `tau ||a||^2 <= ||Ta||^2` for every `tau <= 1`.
- Passage and bypass (under the hypotheses of section 3): for every vector `x` of the span, `passage >= eta * physicalPower x` and `bypass <= delta * physicalPower x` for all large `n` and then all large `r`, where passage is `Re int_D Fact` and bypass is `int_{D^c} |Fact|` for the plane flux density `Fact n r x = E_{n,r} x conj H_{n,r}` of the actual plane fields, through the fixed square `D = Dsq L`. This is the manuscript's `pr:count` with `||Ta||^2 = ||a||^2`.
- No operator of rank below `N` approximates the scattering map on these inputs within `sqrt tau` (`actual_device_no_low_rank`).
- The order of the existential choices is the manuscript's: interpolation family and jet scale from `(L, y, chi)` first (they do not depend on `rho`, `h`); then the height `h` and the ratio `rho = h/2` from the thresholds and the jet scale (`window_choice`); then the enclosing radius; then, for the given `Fact`, `n` large (parity counting and limiting unit-ness), then `r` large (L1 error below the margin `min(eta' - eta, delta - delta')`).

## 3. What is hypothetical

The frozen square theorem `actual_counted_subspaces_square` assumes two statements about an abstract family `Fact : N -> N -> (Index ->0 C) -> Plane -> C` of plane flux densities: (H-hom) `HomogeneousFlux Fact` (`Fact n r (z x) = |z|^2 Fact n r x`) and (H-L1) `L1Interface` (for every orthonormal family, every `n`, every `epsilon > 0`, for all large `r`, on the unit-power sphere of the span, `Fact n r x` and the difference with the parity flux at the matching scale are integrable and the `L1(R^2)` norm of the difference is at most `epsilon`).

Two later modules reduce these two statements to estimates on plane fields:
- `RestrictedTEL1Assembly` (reviews/restricted_te_l1_assembly.md): `L1Interface` follows from measurability, compact `L1` convergence to the parity flux on every disk, and an inverse-cube pointwise majorant of `Fact` (the parity-side integrability and the parity tail majorant uniform on the unit-power sphere, and the homogeneity of the parity flux, are proved there).
- `RestrictedTEActualFields` (reviews/restricted_te_actual_fields.md): for plane fields `E n r`, `H n r` linear in the input (maps `(Index ->0 C) ->l[C] (Plane -> Vector)`), the flux `Fact = E x conj H` (normal component) is homogeneous (proved, by linearity), and `L1Interface` follows from the structure `ActualFieldEstimates`. `actual_counted_subspaces_of_fields` is the counted-subspace statement of section 2 for every such family of fields, with no hypothesis other than `ActualFieldEstimates`.

The three fields of `ActualFieldEstimates (-L) (ysq N L) rho chi h R .. q E H` are exactly the remaining hypotheses:
(i) `meas`: every coordinate of `E n r x` and `H n r x` is a.e.-strongly measurable on the plane;
(ii) `conv`: for every gain index `n`, every unit-power input `x` and every disk, `E n r x` and `H n r x` converge uniformly, as `r -> infinity`, coordinatewise, to the parity total fields `RestrictedTETotalLinear.totalE`/`totalH` of the doubled device (the fields `ParityPlaneFields.totalElectric 1 (q^n) (source x a) (electric (a^-1 x))`, resp. `totalMagnetic`, at the plane point, `a = (q^n)^-1`);
(iii) `tail`: for every orthonormal family and every `n` there are `C, rho1, r1` with `|E n r x xi x conj H n r x xi| <= C/|xi|^3` for `r >= r1`, `|xi| >= rho1`, on the unit-power sphere of the span (equivalently, by `unit_tail_of_pair_tails`, such majorants for the fluxes of fixed pairs of unit-power inputs).

The core module `RestrictedTEActualCore` (reviews/restricted_te_actual_core.md) then discharges (ii) inside the core: the assembled plane fields `coreE`, `coreH` of the combined shell-plus-inclusion solution (the continuous representatives of its total fields, with the weighted data `fsLin x`, `fiLin x` of the incident electric field) are linear in the input and continuous on the open core `|xi| < Rin`; the electric and magnetic backgrounds of the shell equal the regular TE fields of the input rescaled degreewise by `transmission/2` at every core point; the effective datum equals the datum of the rescaled input; and, for all large `n`, the assembled fields converge uniformly on every disk, as the shell index tends to infinity, to the total fields of the parity device with the datum of the input rescaled by `limitingTransmission/2` (`coreE_conv_raw`, `coreH_conv_raw`, `core_conv_raw_eventually`). No extra normalizing factor occurs: the identification with the parity fields at scale `(q^n)^-1` holds exactly when `scaledCoeff x (limitingTransmission/2) = q^n * twist x` for a unitary linear `twist` of the finite input space (`rawLimitE_eq_parity`, `ActualFieldEstimatesTwisted`, `actual_counted_subspaces_of_fields_twisted` for the physical family `A_k = twist^-1 B_k`, `actual_counted_subspaces_of_core`).

What remains for the physical device is exactly: (a) the definition of the actual plane fields `E n r`, `H n r` of the paired-shell device outside the core (the shell region and the exterior; in progress on the fredholm/modal side) as linear maps of the input that agree with the core fields on the open core and are measurable (`CoreAgreement.meas`, `agreeE`, `agreeH`); (b) the open `r`-uniform inverse-cube tail majorant of their flux (`CoreAgreement.tail`); (c) the twist identity `limitingTransmission l (te q hq n) / 2 = q^n * u_l` degreewise with a unitary twist (the modal computation of the limiting transmission; not yet instantiated). Everything else of (i)-(iii) is proved inside the core.

## 4. Remaining differences from the manuscript statement

- The section is the open square `{|x| < L, |y| < L}` centred at the origin (the manuscript's `(-L/2, L/2)^2` with a different parametrization of the half-side, and its closure differs by a null set); all `N` use the same square.
- Frequency: normalized to `k = 1` (the problem is scale-invariant); the support radius grows with `r`, as in the manuscript.
- The finite TE inputs are the even-`l` finite restricted TE fields (input space of the physical power inner product); the manuscript's physical incoming space contains them; no claim is made about the full incoming space.
- Passage here is the real part of the signed section integral of the flux density `Fact`; the identification with `(1/2) Re int_D (E x conj H) . z` is part of the hypothetical identification of the plane fields `E_{n,r}`, `H_{n,r}` with the actual fields of the paired device.
- "Piecewise smooth dielectric" and "vacuum exterior": the actual device is the paired-shell stack (finite radial layers) plus `2N` small spheres, with vacuum outside the stack; the Lean statement is about this coefficient, whose values are proved to lie in `[1, eps_+]`.
