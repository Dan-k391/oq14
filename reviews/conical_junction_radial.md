For the explicit smooth corner function with sharp cone and ball margins, the radial derivative is strictly negative when the margins and slope are positive and the radius is nonnegative. The smooth apex graph has an explicit upper bound. A quantitative condition guarantees that the positive-margin corner region lies outside the cap transition; the condition eventually holds for any nonzero outer radius. The results do not yet establish a global smooth physical boundary, coordinate identification, coefficient convergence, or transmission regularity.
