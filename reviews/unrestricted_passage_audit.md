# Unrestricted passage audit: what power conservation can and cannot bound

Scope: arbitrary finite real positive scalar dielectrics, the fixed section D, physical incoming normalization, and the coherent passage/bypass requirements. Existing source sections are frozen. This file records additional derivations without changing manuscript claims.

## PROVED: unit incoming power does not bound the positive local-flux trace

This conclusion now follows from the actual dielectric synthesis theorem cc:core-gains, without a circuit model or a prescribed-source realization assumption.

Let D be the fixed centered square, with circumradius R_D, vacuum wavenumber k and impedance Z. Choose an odd order l with l(l+1)>k²R_D² and put m=l+1, an even order. Use axisymmetric TE waves with orthonormal spherical harmonics Y_l0,Y_m0 and X_l0=(rhat cross grad_S Y_l0)/sqrt(l(l+1)). Define the nonzero real constants

\[
 c_l=\frac{\partial_\theta Y_{l0}(\pi/2)}{\sqrt{l(l+1)}},
 \qquad d_m=Y_{m0}(\pi/2).
\]

Both regular Bessel factors j_l(kr),j_m(kr) are positive for 0<r<=R_D, by the radial positivity argument in cn:radial-growth. Hence

\[
 I_{lm}=\int_D\frac{j_l(kr)j_m(kr)}r\,dA>0,\qquad
 K_{lm}=4k\sqrt{m(m+1)}\,|c_ld_m|\,I_{lm}>0.
\]

The integral is finite at the origin. A free unit-incoming-power TE wave has electric field 2*k*sqrt(2Z)*j_l(kr)*X_l0. Let a,b be its two physical incoming coefficients, and let g_l,g_m denote the actual core multipliers generated by a radial dielectric.

At z=0 the odd-order TE field has only azimuthal E and normal H; the even-order TE field has zero E there and radial in-plane H. Direct substitution into the physical Poynting form gives

\[
 P_D(a,b)=
 -4k\sqrt{m(m+1)}\,c_ld_m I_{lm}\,
 \operatorname{Im}\{a\overline b\,g_l\overline{g_m}\}.
\]

For clarity, H_r=i*[2*k*sqrt(2Z)/Z]*b*g_m*sqrt(m(m+1))*j_m(kr)*d_m/(kr), and e_phi cross e_r=e_theta=-e_z on the plane; these fix the sign. The Hermitian 2-by-2 section-flux compression therefore has eigenvalues

\[
 \lambda_\pm=\pm\frac{K_{lm}}2|g_lg_m|.
\]

Corollary cc:core-gains supplies actual finite bounded-positive scalar radial dielectrics with g_l,g_m approaching any common positive number G, up to a common phase. Thus their positive flux eigenvalue tends to K_lm*G²/2. Since G is arbitrary, no geometry-independent upper bound exists for:

- the local signed-flux operator norm;
- the trace of its positive part;
- its nuclear norm;
- the trace of its restriction to positive subspaces.

This is an actual finite-dielectric counterexample to deriving any such bound from unit incoming power and losslessness alone. It does not satisfy a fixed bypass bound as G tends to infinity. A formal signed trace may cancel between positive and negative eigenvalues; that cancellation does not control channel dimension.

## PROVED: the positive local-flux index can also be arbitrarily large

The preceding two-mode example is sufficient to refute a universal positive-trace bound. The stronger rank statement below identifies what the bypass hypothesis must exclude.

Fix N and choose distinct q_j>k, with alpha_j=sqrt(q_j²-k²). In cylindrical coordinates define entire regular axisymmetric TE fields

\[
 E_j(\rho,\varphi,z)=J_1(q_j\rho)
 \left[\cosh(\alpha_jz)
       +i\frac{k}{\alpha_j}\sinh(\alpha_jz)\right]e_\varphi,
 \qquad H_j=\frac1{i\omega\mu_0}\nabla\times E_j .
\]

Smoothness at the axis follows from J_1(q rho)/rho being an entire function of rho². These fields have divergence zero; the order-one Bessel equation gives (Delta+k²)E_j=0. Consequently both source-free vacuum Maxwell curl equations hold exactly. On the plane, partial_z E_j=i*k*E_j, so for every coherent a in C^N,

\[
 S_z(a;\rho,0)=\frac1{2Z}
       \left|\sum_{j=1}^Na_jJ_1(q_j\rho)\right|^2.
\]

The associated section Gram matrix is strictly positive. Indeed, a linear combination vanishing on the square vanishes on a radial interval. Its Taylor coefficients at the origin yield the Vandermonde system in the distinct q_j² (after extracting q_j), and hence all coefficients vanish.

Each such entire axisymmetric azimuthal electric field has a convergent regular TE spherical-wave expansion with m=0. A brief justification retaining the approximation required here is as follows. On a sphere of radius R'>R_D, expand its tangential electric trace in X_l0. Projection of the vector Helmholtz equation gives the regular radial solution c_l*j_l(kr) in every order. Choose R' avoiding the finitely many possible low-order zeros of j_l(kR'); all sufficiently large orders are positive there. Smooth angular traces have polynomially decaying coefficients, while j_l(kr)/j_l(kR') is bounded by a constant times (r/R')^l for large l, with polynomial factors for derivatives. Thus the regular-wave series and its first derivatives converge uniformly on a smaller ball containing D. The limit agrees with the original field by the projected radial equations and completeness of the spherical harmonics.

Truncate these N expansions at a common finite order L. Uniform C1 convergence gives convergence of the Hermitian section-flux matrices. For sufficiently large L the truncated N-dimensional TE family still has strictly positive section flux for every nonzero coherent vector. Its coefficient vectors are linearly independent, and, in the physical incoming norm of that finite band, their span V_N has

\[
 P_D^0(a)\geq p_N\|a\|^2,\qquad p_N>0 .
\]

The number p_N may be extremely small; no uniform lower bound is asserted. Apply cc:core-gains with the same real gain G to every TE order up to L. After common rephasing, the actual finite-device flux compression tends to G² times this positive compression. For any prescribed p_*>0 choose G sufficiently large and then a sufficiently accurate finite device. It has

\[
 \dim V_N=N,\qquad P_D(a)\geq p_*\|a\|^2
 \quad(a\in V_N).
\]

Its full physical scattering map is unitary, since the constructed radial dielectric is finite, real and positive. Thus throughput one and coherent positive passage of arbitrary finite rank can coexist in the actual material class. There is still no fixed bypass estimate. The finite-band theorem cb:finitecore in fact forces the bypass budget to increase when N exceeds its stated threshold.

## PROVED: total plane power gives an operator bound only after bypass is imposed

For the finite angular-band radial devices just constructed, plane flux is absolutely integrable. On compact annuli fields are smooth up to finitely many interfaces. At large plane radius the leading spherical-wave Poynting vector is radial and hence tangent to the plane, leaving normal flux O(r^-3).

Real Poynting conservation on the upper half-space gives

\[
 P_\Sigma(a)=P_{\mathrm{out,upper}}(a)
            -P_{\mathrm{in,upper}}(a),\qquad
 |P_\Sigma(a)|\leq\|a\|^2 .
\]

Both hemisphere powers lie between zero and total incoming power because the full scattering map is unitary. The same statement applies to any lossless well-posed finite scatterer with the required integrable plane trace and radiation convention.

It follows for every input that

\[
 \int_{\Sigma\setminus D}|S_z|
 \geq|P_D-P_\Sigma|
 \geq P_D-\|a\|^2.
\]

Thus the unbounded two-mode positive eigenvalue above necessarily produces unbounded outside absolute flux. With a fixed bypass budget delta, power conservation instead gives

\[
 \|Q_D\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq1+\delta
\]

on a subspace obeying bypass for all its vectors. It gives no trace/nuclear bound on that subspace merely by operator algebra: arbitrarily many eigenvalues may lie between the positive passage threshold and 1+delta. The all-input absolute bypass condition contains more spatial information than the signed outside-flux operator, and that information cannot be discarded in an unrestricted proof.

## Exact source diagnostic: localized electric/magnetic reactive circulation

The following uses point dipole sources and is not an admissible frozen-class counterexample. It is a useful exact Maxwell diagnostic of the near-field mechanism that a nearby positive-dielectric transducer would have to realize.

Place an electric dipole p=epsilon_0*a²*P*e_z and a magnetic dipole m=a²*M*e_y at c_a=(0,0,a), with real P,M>0. Let Phi_k(r)=exp(ik|r|)/(4*pi*|r|). Away from c_a their exact outgoing fields are

\[
 E_p=\epsilon_0^{-1}(k^2I+\nabla\nabla)\Phi_k\,p,\qquad
 H_p=-i\omega\nabla\Phi_k\times p,
\]
\[
 H_m=(k^2I+\nabla\nabla)\Phi_k\,m,\qquad
 E_m=i\omega\mu_0\nabla\Phi_k\times m.
\]

They solve both vacuum Maxwell equations off the source. On the scale (x,y)=a(u,v) in the plane, the leading fields are the electric and magnetic static dipole tensors. With R=(u,v,-1), the tensor is [3*R*R^T/|R|²-I]/(4*pi*|R|³). The identity

\[
 (Tp)\times(Tm)=3\widehat R
       [\widehat R\cdot(p\times m)]-2(p\times m),
 \qquad T=3\widehat R\widehat R^T-I,
\]

therefore gives the scaled normal-flux limit

\[
 a^2 S_z(au,av,0)\longrightarrow
 \frac{3PM\,u}{32\pi^2(1+u^2+v^2)^4}.
\]

The integral over u>0 is positive and explicit:

\[
 \int_{u>0}\frac{3PM\,u\,du\,dv}
                   {32\pi^2(1+u^2+v^2)^4}
       =\frac{3PM}{512\pi}.
\]

The u<0 integral is its negative, and the total absolute integral is twice this value. Thus a dipole pair approaching a straight edge of D produces a finite local passage and an equal leading return outside D, although the dipoles and their radiated powers tend to zero.

This asymptotic is also an L1 plane-flux limit, not only pointwise. To check it, use the displayed exact fields: their electrostatic/magnetostatic terms scale as a²/r³; their cross-polarization induction terms as a²/r²; and radiative terms as a²/r. On a fixed near-source region, the nonleading flux integrals are bounded by constants times a, a², a³, or a^4*|log a| and tend to zero. Outside a fixed region the leading radiative cross-Poynting vector is radial; its plane-normal factor is O(a/r), and the remaining terms are O(a^4/r³), giving an integrable vanishing far contribution. The static profile has an integrable tail. Dominated truncation then proves L1 convergence after rescaling. The incident-wave normalization and lossless-dielectric realization are absent here, so the calculation is not promoted to a physical channel construction.

In particular this leading closed-circulation mechanism naturally lives in the regime delta>=p, where p is the positive passage threshold. It cannot by itself settle the stricter delta<p regime. A bounded-positive-dielectric realization still requires a source-free feeding construction, control of induced moments and their relative phases, the response of the changing resonant background, and all-coherent bypass estimates.

## Current conclusion

Actual finite positive dielectrics already defeat any universal positive local-flux trace derived from passivity or full unit-power scattering alone. The bypass condition is indispensable. The paired-control theorem strengthens suppression of selected opposite-wall responses, but does not estimate a complete Green operator or nearby perturbation. The unrestricted channel target remains open; no prescribed-source or local-field diagnostic above is asserted to close it.
