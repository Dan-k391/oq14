For actual regional weak Maxwell fields with bounded dielectric, every nonzero-step coordinate difference quotient of the compactly supported magnetic cutoff is L²-bounded uniformly by an explicit expression involving the cutoff, frequency, dielectric bound, and regional electric and magnetic L² norms. The derivative bound is derived from the existing weak Maxwell H¹ energy estimate. This does not bound difference quotients of curl H or prove H² or boundary regularity.
