The module makes the plane flux density of one actual TM source mode explicit in the degree. For an arbitrary nonzero point `x` and the layered datum `d` (all layer permittivities at least `1`), the electric field splits exactly as `E = (ps / r^(l+2)) * poloidal(P)(n) + (l(l+1) u / (sigma r^(l+3))) * P(n) * position`, with `(u, p) = raw d r` the actual radial state at `r = ||x||` and `n` the unit direction, and the magnetic field is the purely tangential vector `-(I u / r^(l+1)) * angularToroidal(P)(n)` (`electric_split`, `magnetic_eq`, with the layer reciprocal `1/sigma` bounded by `1` through `inverseSigma_norm_le`). From this the band decomposition `SourceRemotePlaneBand.Decomp` is obtained with explicit constants in the state magnitudes: orthogonal part of the electric field at most `||p|| * Mpol / r`, parallel part at most `l(l+1) ||u|| * Mp / r^2`, orthogonal part of the magnetic field at most `||u|| * Mt / r`, no parallel part (`tm_decomp_state`), where `Mpol, Mp, Mt` are any sphere bounds for the poloidal, scalar and toroidal angular factors of the homogeneous polynomial `P`. The diagonal plane flux density follows (`plane_flux_density_state`, via `flux_bound_of_decomp`).

Normalization of `P`: in the all-degree statements `P` is `representative l m`, the harmonic polynomial attached to the `m`-th element of the orthonormal real basis of degree-`l` spherical harmonics (unit `L^2` norm on the unit sphere). Its angular factors are bounded through the angular envelope `AngularExteriorBounds.envelope l = scale * (l+1)^3 * base^l` (poloidal by `(l+1) * envelope`, scalar and radial by `envelope`, toroidal by `2 * envelope`). `degree_flux_bound` gives `|flux density| <= 4 l(l+1) envelope(l)^2 ||u||^2 / ||x||^3` at every plane point with `x 2 = 0`, `x != 0`, for any layered datum; `te_degree_flux_eq` shows the TE dual has the identical modulus (`teElectric = magnetic`, `teMagnetic = -electric` through `SourceRemotePlaneFluxTE.te_flux_eq_conj`). Barrier range: with `2 q R <= l`, `R <= ||x||`, `d.X <= 2 d.R`, layer wave numbers at most `q`, and an inner scale `0 < s <= R`, the geometric source-weighted bound of `BarrierAnnulusStateBounds.source_weighted_all_high` (valid at every radius at least the inner radius, annulus and exterior together) gives `(regular l s)^2 * |flux density| <= barrierConstant R s l / ||x||^3` with `barrierConstant R s l = 16 R^2 exp(R) envelope(l)^2 (s/R)^(2(l+1)) = 4 barrierAmplitude^2` (`degree_flux_barrier`, `barrierConstant_eq`). Summability: for `base * s < R` the series `(l+1)^k * barrierAmplitude` and `(l+1)^k * barrierConstant` are summable, and the multiplicity-weighted series `(2l+1) (l+1)^k barrierConstant` is summable with nonnegative sum (`summable_barrierAmplitude`, `summable_barrierConstant`, `tsum_degree_flux`), by comparison with `(l+1)^j rho^(l+1)`, `rho < 1`.

Not proved: any bound outside the barrier range (low and intermediate degrees are treated in `BarrierDegreeDecomp`), any bound on the sphere factors of an arbitrary `P` other than the `representative` basis, a lower bound on the flux, the interchange of the degree sum with the flux integral, and anything about the incident or background field. The constant `barrierConstant R s l` depends on the shell radius `R` through `R^2 exp(R) (s/R)^(2(l+1))`; making it uniform over the shell index requires the threshold radius device of `BarrierDegreeDecomp`. All hypotheses are the stated layered, degree-range and radius hypotheses; no estimate is assumed.
