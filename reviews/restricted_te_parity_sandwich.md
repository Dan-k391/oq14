Construction. `ballCoefficient R` is the indicator coefficient of the closed ball of radius R (bound one) with `ballSupport R` its support data (carrier the ball). `reactionBall R e k := BoundedMaterialKernel.operator (ballSupport R) (ParityReactionVolume.entries e k)` is therefore the literal kernel integral operator `S 1_B * K_e * S 1_B`, the compression of the wall-reflection kernel `SphereReactionKernel.reaction e k` to the ball; it depends only on R, e, k. `parityKernel R k B := ShellInclusionVolume.kernel k R + (B^2-1) • reactionBall R true k + (B⁻¹^2-1) • reactionBall R false k` is a bounded operator on VectorL2.

Proved: `operator_sandwich`: `ParityReactionVolume.operator d e k = sqrtMultiplier c * reactionBall d.radius e k * sqrtMultiplier c` for every coefficient c with support data d (via the a.e. component formula `BoundedMaterialKernel.component_operator_ae` on both sides, the a.e. formula for `sqrtMultiplier`, the fact that the compressed operator sees `sqrtMultiplier c u` unchanged because it vanishes off the ball, and `physical_ext`). `block_sandwich`: `ParityMaterialField.block d k B = 1 - sqrtMultiplier c * parityKernel d.radius k B * sqrtMultiplier c`. `block_eq_shell`: `block d k B = ShellInclusionBlocks.shell (sqrtMultiplier c) (parityKernel d.radius k B)`, so root's shell algebra applies with the parity device as the shell. `addSupport` gives support data for the sum of two coefficients with a common radius, and `block_add_eq_combined` shows that for disjoint cs, ci the parity block of the sum equals `ShellInclusionBlocks.combined (S cs) (S ci) (parityKernel R k B)`, with the same T_B as the shell.

Not proved: no invertibility or smallness of any of these blocks, no effective-block or inverse statement for the parity device (the corresponding unit/inverse statements follow from ShellInclusionBlocks once hypotheses on the shell block and the effective block are supplied), no identification of T_B with a physical finite-stack Green kernel (rg:physical-limit), and no claim that the compression is the restriction of a bounded operator on all of L2(R^3) (it is not, the entire reaction kernel is unbounded there).
