Proved: (1) the difference of the actual complete parity flux form and the exact finite-dipole flux form is quadratically homogeneous, diffForm(z*A, z*A) = z*(conj z * diffForm(A, A)) (from its sesquilinearity). (2) For any finite family A_j of fixed coefficient vectors and eps > 0, for all sufficiently small a > 0 and simultaneously for every c with sum |c_j|^2 <= 1, the flux difference of the actual inverse for the input sum c_j A_j is integrable with whole-plane L1 mass at most eps. The proof takes the finite index set s to be the union of the supports, bounds every entry of the combination by K = 1 + sum of all entries of the A_j on s, applies the sup-norm polydisc theorem `uniform_form` to the rescaled vector (1/K) sum c_j A_j with eps/K^2, and rescales by the quadratic homogeneity. (3) If the physical incoming inputs `RestrictedTEInput.input (A j)` are orthonormal, the same holds for every element of the complex span of the A_j with `physicalPower <= 1`, because `input_norm_sq : ||input A||^2 = physicalPower A` and orthonormality give physicalPower(sum c_j A_j) = sum |c_j|^2.

Not proved: the hypotheses are those of `actual_inverse_to_dipoles` (actual geometry, separation, containment, height gap); the finite family and eps are fixed before a is taken small, so nothing is uniform in the family or in the number of channels; the replacement of the ball-center field values by the plane jets of the interpolated inputs, the explicit section integrals of the dipole flux over D and its complement, the physical finite-stack Green identification, outgoing-power normalization and the counted-channel threshold choice are all separate (see reviews/channel_closure_scope_2026_10_24.md).
