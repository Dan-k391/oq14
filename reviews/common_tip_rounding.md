The resulting coefficient is smooth in each such ball and converges AE to the original since the finite set of centers is null. Existing material-volume stability then gives global L2 field convergence under its explicit uniform common-frequency-radius condition. This does not establish globally smooth interfaces, hotspot peaks, or a single illumination.
