A square-root multiplier sandwich has operator norm at most the coefficient bound times the norm of the middle operator, since the multiplier norm is at most the square root of the bound. Hence the inclusion-weighted reaction operator is bounded by the inclusion contrast bound times the scattered-Green norm, and the scattered-Green norm by the kernel norm squared times the shell bound times the shell inverse norm. The effective inclusion block is the positive static weighted block of the inclusion minus the dynamic remainder plus the reaction operator; the smallness quantity is the frozen dynamic error bound of the inclusion plus contrast times scattered-Green norm. When it is below one, the frozen positive-block perturbation theorem makes the effective block a unit with inverse norm at most one over one minus the smallness, and the combined shell-plus-inclusion block is then a unit whenever the shell block is.
