The class is exactly that of the existing `RadialFlux.maxwell_weighted_flux`: a measurable real permittivity bounded by M, a field pair with local L2 components on every ball, and `MaterialMaxwell.TestMaxwell k eps E H` (the weak Maxwell equations against all compactly supported test functions on all of space). For every C1 profile theta vanishing beyond some radius, the real compact test x to theta(norm x ^ 2) is fed to the existing `MaterialPower.testMaxwell_power`; polar coordinates (`PolarIntegration.integral_polar`, `RadialFlux.integrable_polar`) and the pointwise ray identity give integrability and vanishing of the integral over positive radii of 2 r theta'(r^2) times the sphere flux `RadialFlux.flux E H r` (`weak_radial`). For any continuous weight g with compact support inside the positive reals, the primitive of g(sqrt s)/(2 sqrt s) is such a theta whose profile is exactly g, so the integral of g times the flux over positive radii vanishes and the integrand is integrable (`weak_smooth`). Hence the flux is locally integrable on the open positive half-line (`flux_locallyIntegrableOn`, with a bump weight equal to one near each point), and Mathlib's open-set fundamental lemma gives `flux_ae_zero`: the flux is zero for almost every positive radius. The proposed constancy statement follows with constant zero (`flux_ae_constant`, for any R0 nonnegative).

Adjustment relative to the scope note: the existing weak-power identity is global (the weak equations hold on all of space), so the conclusion is stronger than constancy, namely that the constant is zero, and the proof needs no separate argument that the constant vanishes. Constancy between two radii in a lossless annulus when the weak equations hold only there would need a regional radial test (the regional power machinery takes regional Hcurl representatives, not the global TestMaxwell class used here); that regional statement is not proved. The statements hold for almost every radius, not every radius, since no continuity of the flux is proved. For the total field of a regular incident wave plus scattered wave, with real bounded permittivity, this is the vanishing of the total radial flux; it does not by itself give the unit-throughput identity, because that still needs the split into incoming and outgoing parts and the cross terms. No hypothesis assumes the flux conclusion.
