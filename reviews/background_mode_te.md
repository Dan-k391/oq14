TE duality and radiating pairs. For a stack with unit `sigma` on every layer (the TE data of the physical stack) the TE background pair is the dual of the transverse-magnetic structure of the same radial state: electric field `backgroundMagnetic d P`, magnetic field `-backgroundElectric d P`. `te_background_forced` proves, from the proved TE source correction `SourceTEGlobal.forced_maxwell` (the TE pair is `(H_c, -E_c)`), its conjugate pair and the regular vacuum mode (`teVacuumPair`), that this pair is a weak forced pair for `teBackgroundPolarization d P = kappa * (rho_te + conj rho_te)` (the `sum` with the conjugate pair, because the TE electric field carries the factor `I` from the toroidal formula), and `teBackgroundPolarization_eq` identifies the force with `(mu - 1) * backgroundMagnetic` (`conj_mu`: `mu` is real). `RadiatingPair R E H rho` bundles all hypotheses of `RadiatingSourceRepresentation.actual_response` (forced equation, local integrability and square integrability, exterior smoothness, the radiation trace, integrable square integrable force vanishing outside the ball); it is closed under scalar multiples, sums and finite sums (`smul`, `add`, `sum_nonempty`, with the zero pair `zeroTrace`), and `RadiatingPair.representation` gives the volume representation of a pair. `tePair` builds the pair of the TE scattered part of one mode (`scatteredMagnetic`, `-scatteredElectric`; smoothness `scatteredMagnetic_smooth`, trace `SourceRadiatingTrace.dual_trace (scatteredTrace ...)`).

Finite families and finite coefficient vectors. For `A : Index ->0 C`, `teCoeff A q = coefficient A q * I` (with `coefficient A q = A q * 4 pi I^l`) and `RestrictedTEPlaneLocal.electric A = famIncident A.support (teCoeff A) (q.val.1) polynomial` (`electric_eq_famIncident`, from `teElectric (wave l 1 P) = I * SolidMaxwellProfiles.magnetic l P`, `teElectric_wave`; `electric_single` records the basis modes). `te_finite_datum`: the family incident datum is `RestrictedTEMaterial.datum` for the support datum of the ball of radius `X`; `te_finite_response`: the total field of the actual shell solution for the restricted-TE datum of `A` is the family background a.e.; `te_finite_response_parity` states the same with the parity objects at `B = 1` (`parity_solution_one`, `parity_total_one`). `te_finite_core`: for `x != 0`, `||x|| <= R`, the family background equals `RestrictedTEPlaneLocal.electric (scaledCoeff A (fun l => transmission l d0.gs d0.R d0.X d0.hR / 2)) x`, matching the normalization `scaledCoeff` of `BackgroundCoreTransmission` with the factor `1/2` for the unit regular incident wave.
