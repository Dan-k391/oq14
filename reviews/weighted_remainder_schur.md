Proved: for a coefficient `c` with support data `d` (carrier `K`), supported in the closed ball of radius `R`, any measurable set `E` containing every difference `x - y` of carrier points on which the remainder kernel entries `DyadicKernel.remainder k` are integrable, and any `M` bounding every entrywise L1 mass `int_E ||remainder k z i j||`, one has `||DynamicVolume.weightedRemainder c k R|| <= 9 * c.bound * M`. The proof replaces the remainder kernel by its restriction to `E` (`kappa`), shows the weighted sandwich equals the sandwich of the convolution with any integrable kernel matrix agreeing with the remainder on carrier differences (`weightedRemainder_eq`, using the pointwise form `weightedRemainder_actual`, the `StaticNewton` source representation and the carrier-zero property), and bounds that convolution by the Young inequality entry by entry (`opG_norm`, a `3 x 3` block operator of `L1Convolution.operator` entries).

Deviation from the requested form: the hypothesis is the L1 mass of the kernel on a set `E` containing `K - K`, not a supremum over `x` of an integral over the carrier (a literal Schur row-sum). The mass form is the one that is carrier-sensitive for a union of small balls, since `E` is then a finite union of small balls.

Not proved: no explicit value of `M` for any specific carrier (that is the content of the partner smallness module); no lower bound; no bound on the full dynamic operator, only on the remainder sandwich.
