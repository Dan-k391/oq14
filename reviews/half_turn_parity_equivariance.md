The scalar Fourier equivalence commutes with the half-turn pullback: on Schwartz functions the pullback is composition with the linear isometry, the Fourier integral satisfies mathlib's `fourierIntegral_comp_linearIsometry`, and equality extends to all of L2 by density of the Schwartz range. This gives commutation of the actual vector Fourier equivalence (and its inverse) with `U`, using that the vector Fourier transform acts componentwise. The longitudinal Fourier multiplier commutes with `U`: at frequency xi the symbol is the projection onto the line of xi, whose coordinate formula is covariant under the diagonal sign matrix (the sign factors square to one and the denominator is unchanged). Hence the physical longitudinal projection, a conjugate of that multiplier by the Fourier equivalence, commutes with `U`, and no hypothesis on the physical projection is used (the hypothesis named `hP` in the scoping message is not needed).

Combining with the equivariance of the square-root multiplier, the dynamic remainder sandwich and the parity reaction operators from `HalfTurnVectorAction`, the actual parity material block of a half-turn symmetric coefficient commutes with `U` for every frequency and every parity ratio. If the block is a unit (or the existing smallness bound makes it one), the inverse preserves `U`-fixed data: the parity solution of half-turn-fixed data is half-turn fixed, and its weighted source is almost everywhere half-turn invariant. Not proved here: invertibility of the block (an explicit hypothesis or the smallness hypothesis), and any statement that a particular physical datum is half-turn fixed.
