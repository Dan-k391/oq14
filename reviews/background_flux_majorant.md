Mode fields as functions of the radial state. For a layered datum `d` the background fields are exactly the electric and magnetic mode formulas applied to the half background state `bgHalf d r = (1/2) * backgroundState d.l d.gs d.R d.X d.hR r`, which equals `kappa d * (S - conj S)` for the source state `S` (`bgHalf_eq`): `backgroundElectric d P x i = Eform d.l P (inverseSigma d ||x||) (bgHalf d ||x||).1 (bgHalf d ||x||).2 x i` and `backgroundMagnetic d P x i = Hform d.l P (bgHalf d ||x||).1 x i` for `x != 0` (`backgroundElectric_eq_form`, `backgroundMagnetic_eq_form`, from the conjugation identities of `BackgroundModeTE`). The radial and tangential decomposition of the audited TM mode (`BarrierDegreeFluxConstants.tm_decomp_state`, tied to `raw d`) is re-proved verbatim for an arbitrary state `(u,p)` and inverse permittivity `s` with `||s|| <= 1` (`eform_split`, `hform_eq`, `eh_decomp_state`): the electric formula splits into a part orthogonal to `x` of size `||p|| Mpol / r` and a radial part of size `l(l+1) ||u|| Mp / r^2`, the magnetic formula is orthogonal to `x` of size `||u|| Mt / r`, where `Mpol, Mp, Mt` bound the poloidal, scalar and toroidal angular factors on the sphere (`sphere_bounds`, by compactness).

Uniform state bound. `bgHalf_energy_le`: for a stack with `1 <= sigma <= M`, `q^2 <= M` on every layer, a transmission bound `T`, width `X - R <= W`, `angular l <= A R^2` and every radius `r >= rho` with `angular l <= rho^2/4`, the energy `|u|^2 + |p|^2` of `bgHalf d r` is at most `(T/2)^2 (16/3) + (1/4) exp((2M + A) W) (64/3) + 64/3`. Core (`r <= R`): the state is `(transmission/2)` times the regular state and its energy is at most the Frobenius energy `<= 16/3` of the low-range bound (`HankelRealBasis.low_range_frobeniusSq` with `c = 1/2`, which is why only radii `r >= rho` with `angular l <= rho^2/4` are used). Annulus: the state is `(1/2)` times the glued profile of the exterior combination `incoming + reflection * outgoing` at `X` (`|reflection| = 1`), whose energy at `X` is at most `2 (16/3 + 16/3)`, propagated layer by layer (`BarrierAnnulusStateBounds.profile_energy_le`) over a length at most the stack width. Exterior: the state is the regular state plus `(reflection - 1)/2` times the outgoing state, with `|(reflection - 1)/2| <= 1`, so energy at most `2 (16/3 + 16/3)`. The bound is independent of the shell index.
