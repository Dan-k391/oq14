# Independent audit of the unrestricted channel counterexample

The canonical statement is `uc:counterexample` in
`manuscript/sections/channel_unrestricted.tex`. Root wrote the final
integration after independently reading both analytic bridges. The channel
and hotspot agents then independently read the final statement and proof.
Both reported no substantive defect in its quantifiers, normalization,
diagonal choices, geometric admissibility or coherent inequalities.

The fixed parameters are k0>0, epsilon_plus>1, L>0, tau in (0,1), and
delta>eta>0. The full lossless scattering map is selected as output.
This is an allowed instance of the frozen definition, not a theorem about
a separately prescribed receiving hemisphere. The section is the same
square for every device. A circulation counterexample does not settle a
reformulation requiring smaller bypass or a sole topological connection.

For each N, exact finite-band electric-wall jets and Gram balancing give
orthonormal physical incoming data. Their finite Gram number lambda may
depend on N. The common stack's actual gain is B=q^M, and a=t*sqrt(lambda)/B
gives the required jet amplitude t/a without changing incoming power.
All constants in the small-inclusion estimate are uniform in a for this
fixed N. The source norm and reaction feedback are genuinely controlled
by the positive static inverse and B squared times sphere volume = O(a).

The radial source proof treats every angular order. In particular it does
not infer source response from incoming gain. The exact outgoing source
Wronskian, positive barrier and both-state outward monotonicity control
large orders. Exterior turning-point bounds and growing-core Herglotz
tails give absolute whole-plane convergence. At fixed a and B, inverse
stability identifies the converging sources with the actual dielectric
polarizations. The radius is chosen only after those quantities are fixed.

The combined whole-plane L1 error is uniform for every coherent unit
coefficient vector. Since the model passage and bypass both tend to a
fixed p0 strictly between eta and delta, a single sufficiently small a
and finite radius work for the entire N-dimensional subspace. Unit
throughput follows from the full lossless scattering map. Each device
has finitely many smooth shells and spheres, common scalar bounds, finite
support, no impressed internal source, and a positive vacuum gap around D.

This is an analytic, AI-assisted independent review, not human peer review
or full Lean verification. The numerical diagnostics do not simulate this
many-channel family. The full written Maxwell construction remains a
formalization obligation.
