\begin{abstract}
We investigate the two conjectures in Section 5.6 of Miller and Monticone's versioned tutorial. We prove simultaneous driven hotspot packing in an invariant two-dimensional setting and in finite three-dimensional dielectric devices, under explicit localization and incoming-power conventions. The three-dimensional result uses conical point tips and a fixed small positive contrast. The primary channel target fixes frequency, material bounds, the physical section, and power, passage, and absolute-bypass thresholds, with unrestricted external support and fine structure. We prove resource-dependent vector estimates and a local coherent-flux obstruction, but do not resolve the unrestricted physical channel target. The modal hotspot interpretation also remains open. Ten operator and integral theorems are checked in Lean; the continuum Maxwell arguments are not formalized.
\end{abstract}
