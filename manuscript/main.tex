\begin{abstract}
We investigate the two conjectures in Section 5.6 of Miller and Monticone's versioned tutorial. The primary channel target fixes frequency, positive dielectric bounds, the physical section, and power, passage, and absolute-bypass thresholds while permitting arbitrarily fine structure and unrestricted exterior support. We separate restricted hotspot theorems, full-vector field-resource estimates, and exact counterexamples to intermediate lemmas. In particular, absolute bypass control connects sharp-section passage to a smooth flux observable. No unrestricted full-Maxwell, fixed-section channel bound or admissible many-channel counterexample is claimed. Model calculations are distinguished from continuum Maxwell constructions throughout.
\end{abstract}
