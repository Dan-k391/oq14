\begin{abstract}
We investigate the two conjectures in Section 5.6 of Miller and Monticone's versioned tutorial. We prove simultaneous driven hotspot packing in invariant two-dimensional and finite three-dimensional dielectric devices. We also prove arbitrary finite packing in one genuinely normalized quasinormal mode of a finite smooth dielectric, at fixed real frequency, common ordinary material bounds, and arbitrarily high prescribed quality factor. The modal proof combines a simple sphere pole, analytic continuation of conical coefficients, and a reciprocal residue norm stable under rounding. The primary channel target fixes frequency, material bounds, the physical section, and power, passage, and absolute-bypass thresholds, with unrestricted external support and fine structure. Resource-dependent vector estimates and additional exact obstructions are proved, but the unrestricted physical channel target remains open. Lean checks operator estimates and classical and weak Maxwell identities; the complete continuum existence and spectral arguments are not yet formalized.
\end{abstract}
