\begin{abstract}
We study quantified versions of two questions on field concentration and
power channels posed by Miller and Monticone in OQ14. At a fixed driving
frequency and under a common positive permittivity bound, we construct
arbitrarily many simultaneous localized electric-field peaks in a single
driven field, both in a two-dimensional invariant setting and in finite
three-dimensional dielectrics. We also construct arbitrarily many localized
peaks in a single normalized outgoing mode of a finite smooth dielectric,
with prescribed real part of the complex resonance frequency and an
arbitrarily high quality-factor target. The modal normalization is a
reciprocal-residue normalization.
Its relation to other possible interpretations of the original modal-volume
question requires separate justification.

For a specified channel definition, we construct, for every finite $N$,
an $N$-dimensional counted input space through the same square section
at fixed frequency, material bounds, and thresholds. The construction
applies when the permitted absolute-bypass threshold exceeds the required
passage threshold and when the selected output is the complete outgoing
scattering map, including reflection. Thus the corresponding unrestricted
uniform finiteness assertion fails. The result does not decide stricter
bypass conditions or receiving-only output formulations. These quantified
results do not resolve every interpretation of the original OQ14. The
formal verification and its scope are described in Appendix~\ref{ver:lean}.
\end{abstract}
