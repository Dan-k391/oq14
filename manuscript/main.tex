\section{Baseline}
This project attempts the two conjectures in Section 5.6 of Miller and Monticone, arXiv:2605.24738v1. No resolution is asserted at this baseline. The physical class has fixed positive frequency, real scalar permittivity $1\leq\varepsilon_r\leq\varepsilon_+$, and nonmagnetic reciprocal materials. The channel target fixes the physical section, exterior conventions, positive efficiency and passage thresholds, and an absolute bypass tolerance. Hotspot packing, driven versus modal normalization, and two- versus three-dimensional realizations are distinct targets.
\section{Research plan}
Audit the source and earlier arguments; split hotspot construction, full-device channel bounds, and physical counterexamples; prove stable auxiliary results; identify every continuum bridge; verify numerical diagnostics separately from proofs; compile and review the integrated manuscript; deliver reproducible source and Git history.
