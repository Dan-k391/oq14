Independent root source review: quantitative regional forcing bootstrap

The explicit second forcing derivative retains all terms: mixed cutoff
products of the original F, first matrix derivatives times first cutoff
products, and the second matrix derivative times the cutoff correction.
Only tangential differentiation is used through the discontinuous flat
weight. No derivative of the material jump in the normal direction is
discarded. Compact cutoff derivatives through order three and matrix
derivatives through order two are bounded explicitly.

RegionalForcingUniform obtains the actual mixed scalar derivative from the
previous regional tangential quotient theorem. The starting hypotheses
are the regional divergence equation, the actual first scalar gradient,
and the original forcing derivatives. It does not assume the original
scalar is H2 on the outer cutoff annulus. The intermediate derivative is
bounded by the regional energy envelope before insertion into the second
forcing estimate. All coefficients of ceiling replacements are proved
nonnegative, and the displayed bound depends only on the original data
ceilings and the fixed chart and cutoff constants.

RegionalBootstrapUniform uses the literal cutScalar, localizedGradient,
localizedForcing and cutFluxSource definitions. The global divergence is
retained with its actual source; the source is zero only on the specified
inner plateau. It does not claim global zero divergence. Local first and
second forcing derivatives are genuine weak derivatives of these exact
fields. Weak derivative uniqueness identifies the witnesses from the
regional construction with the chosen first forcing arrays. A single max
ceiling controls U, all G/R components and both tangential forcing arrays.

The six modules are independently rebuilt with lean.exe directly, isolated
fresh objects first in LEAN_PATH and dependency order checked. Frozen
author hashes are checked before and after compilation, all declared axiom
reports must occur, and only standard axioms and warning-free output are
accepted. Upstream objects outside this batch are cached; this is not a
whole-project clean build. Full actual collar composition remains a
separate theorem and is not supplied merely by these input estimates.
