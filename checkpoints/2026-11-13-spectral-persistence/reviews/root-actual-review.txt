Independent root review of thirteen Terra-authored scalar/material modules

No mathematical findings. The complex simple-zero proofs use uniform derivative
convergence on compact balls, a strict contraction with a proved invariant ball,
and uniqueness; the selected sequence converges by comparing the same unique
root with arbitrarily near roots. Scalar normalization divides only by a proved
nonzero derivative. The repaired operator is invertible on a common neighborhood,
and its inverses and scalar Schur functions converge locally uniformly there.
SchurModeLimit evaluates those inverses along the same convergent roots, obtaining
convergent nonzero normalized kernel vectors, exact one-dimensional kernels, and
nonzero derivative crossing. These are conditional operator-family theorems;
they do not assume an inverse pole, but they require the displayed norm convergence,
analyticity, rank-one repair, and simple base crossing.

The actual material chain separately establishes both unweighted and weighted
compact-part convergence. The weighted proof uses bounded-a.e. sqrt-multiplier
strong convergence, selfadjointness, a common support-ball cutoff, compact left
and right composition, and a uniformly bounded static inverse. It does not
incorrectly claim multiplier operator-norm convergence. Actual weighted pencils
converge locally uniformly in frequency. MaterialHolomorphic similarly gives
analyticity and local uniform convergence for the unweighted formulation.

The root-authored sphere reduction and pole formulas have separate independent
Terra reviews and fresh isolated builds, copied alongside this review. Root's
ordered combined build uses fresh objects for all 23 modules and cached objects
for 707 authenticated upstream local sources. No whole-project clean build.

Theorems do not yet instantiate an actual rounded material family preserving
the sector, nor prove modal hotspot excitation, curved-interface maxima/collars,
or completed full OQ14. Later symmetry and physical normalization convergence
modules remain outside this checkpoint.
