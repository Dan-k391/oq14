Geometry is chosen after the specific nonzero normalized sphere mode: fixed aperture 3/4, positive literal cone contrast n², positive radii inside smooth cutoff and sphere, and pairwise separated on-axis centers. Branch uses positive eta=1/(j+1), selects that exact base mode before geometry, and preserves the certified mode, normalization, high-Q property and response convergence eventually. ScaledLocal transfers the explicit sphere interior field under scalar normalization using the actual L² response identity. These results alone do not establish conical excitation, rounded persistence, or full ambient kernel simplicity. Fresh objects use cached upstream imports.
