The actual physical complex electric representative is identified with the
material response only on the respective containing balls. The component
identity is obtained from the proved normalized weighted-kernel placement,
with source support and reciprocalNorm=1 supplied explicitly. Both the
forward and inverse affine rotation-dilation maps preserve null sets.

The Euclidean physical amplitude is a square root of the sum of three
component norm squares; the cyclic permutation and real positive factor
sqrt(s)*s are proved exactly. No use is made of the Pi sup norm as a proxy
for this amplitude. The unit-image specialization derives containment in
the target observation ball and uses the strictly positive susceptibility.

Actual one-sided representatives are transported component by component.
The restricted-measure endpoint is on the literal image of a measurable
base phase contained in the unit interior, not on its boundary by an AE
claim. Boundary traces must be transported using continuity on the phase
closure, which is supplied separately by the root geometry and hotspot
modules. No collar bound, peak existence, or smoothness is assumed proved
by these two identification modules.

The root build uses a direct lean.exe invocation with the fresh object
directory first in LEAN_PATH; the two reviewed modules are rebuilt in
dependency order. Other upstream objects are cached. The reviewer did not
author these two modules. Source hashes are checked against the author's
frozen manifest, and all emitted declarations and standard-only axiom
reports are checked. This is not a whole-project clean build.
