# Independent graph-forcing bounds review

The five source and author-log hashes match the frozen graph-forcing author-build.json. A fresh direct pinned-compiler build used retained objects in author dependency order, with reviewed nested-chart, localized, quantitative and tangential dependencies ahead of cached objects. Five modules and eight public reports passed using only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound; no warnings, placeholders, missing theorem reports or custom axioms were found. Exact paths, commands, timings and hashes are in independent-build.json.

QuantitativeBinaryForcing constructs the literal binary weighted forcing and its first and second tangential weak derivatives from assumed compact P and full weak derivative arrays, retaining the (1+d) norm factor. GraphH2Norm establishes bounded multiplier and slope norms, then controls the actual pullback gradient and weak Hessian using the stated graph slopes and slope-derivative bounds. MultiplierH2Norm applies the product rule with explicitly bounded multiplier and its first two derivatives.

QuantitativeGraphPiola applies those weak H² estimates to the actual transformed flux, retaining compactness, weak derivative identities and explicit bounds from Q/QG/QH and fixed graph constants. UniformGraphForcing composes this with the binary weight to produce the literal forcing and genuine tangential derivative arrays with bounds depending only on the given input norm and graph constants.

The uniform estimates assume common bounds on compact Q/QG/QH and on the fixed graph slopes and derivatives. They do not by themselves derive those common inputs from the actual rounded family, select a finite collar cover, or establish the final uniform physical collar estimate.
