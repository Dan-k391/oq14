Independent root semantic review of the uniform cone zero-pair chain

All twelve source proofs reviewed. No defect found. The angular weak equation
forces the constant correction moments B0=lambda*A0. Its exact radial
particular solution is -r*A0, which cancels the r/lambda*B0 contribution to
the total-field projection. Subtracting this constant before estimation gives
a remainder controlled by Holder increments, uniformly as the singular
exponent approaches one. The bounds require alpha>0 and, for the total
projection, 1/2<=nu<1. Angular weights are bounded uniformly at a fixed
aperture and bounded nonnegative contrast; no uniformity as the aperture
approaches its endpoints is asserted.

The correction moment increment estimate follows from actual angular
integration and the assumed Holder bound for W(x)-W(0). The intrinsic
expansion then shows that a zero coefficient forces the actual total
projection to be O(r^(1+alpha)). Exact polar integration identifies the
annular total-field pairing with lambda times the radial projected integral.
Its norm is bounded by C M integral r^(2+alpha)|zeta|, uniformly for the
stated branch. Complex scalar pairing is continuous in both L2 arguments.

These are actual System identities, but the common Holder bound for the
physical Green correction is still an input. The estimates do not by
themselves prove that an actual Maxwell mode has nonzero excitation: the
strict limiting-pair comparison and actual mode/weight convergence remain.
They do not assert curved regularity, attained hotspots or full OQ14 closure.
