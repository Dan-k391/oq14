`response_inner_ae` identifies each component of the actual local sphere response almost everywhere on the open unit ball with the explicit interior dipole field, by canceling the strictly positive square-root material contrast. The statement requires n>1, nonzero complex frequency, the TM determinant zero, and observation radius at least one. `axial_nonzero_near_center` proves a genuine open ball with nonzero axial component for this explicit interior field whenever its scalar amplitude and frequency are nonzero. Its conclusion applies to that continuous explicit representative; the preceding response identification is almost everywhere. No nonzero coupling to the inserted cone, or pointwise identity of the L² representative, is claimed.
