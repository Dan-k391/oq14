# Independent semantic review

Both sources were read and rebuilt to fresh objects, without diagnostics. The two public reports use only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound; source hashes were unchanged across each build.

`admissible` constructs the actual rounded and sharp coefficient families. It proves a common coefficient bound, almost-everywhere convergence, support in the unit ball, and axial and meridional symmetry, subject to its explicit geometry, base-support and symmetry hypotheses. Its eta is nonnegative and may be zero; strict positivity must come from a caller.

`normalized_rounded_modes` applies the certified perturbation theorem to this same sharp coefficient and its rounded family. Given the certified, physically normalized limiting mode and its positive real and negative imaginary frequency, it obtains convergent selected-sector fields and local responses, and eventually actual outgoing modes with the same physical reciprocal normalization, observed pole certificate, and the stated strict Q bound. The statement is conditional on the base certificate, geometry and chosen strict Q margin; it does not establish a nonzero cone excitation or attained hotspots.
